## Why Choose Private Spanish Lessons? {#why-private}

- **All ages and levels welcome:** We teach elementary school students, middle and high school students preparing for exams or building proficiency, and adults learning Spanish for work, travel, or personal growth.
- **Flexible scheduling:** Choose days and times that fit your family or work routine. Daytime, after-school, and evening slots are available, making private lessons ideal for homeschool students and busy professionals.
- **In-person or online:** Meet your instructor at our main Kearny Mesa location ([4550 Kearny Villa Rd, Suite 202](/location/)), at our Encinitas location, at your home by arrangement, or online from anywhere via Zoom.
- **Personalized curriculum:** Lessons are customized around your goals. From beginner vocabulary and grammar fundamentals to reading comprehension, conversational confidence, or AP Spanish preparation, your teacher focuses on what matters to you.
- **Qualified native instructors:** Learn with native Spanish educators who have specialized academic degrees in language teaching and modern languages, as well as proven classroom experience with both children and adults.

## Private Lessons for Children & Teens {#kids}

Private instruction is ideal for young learners who thrive with individual attention, as well as students needing targeted support alongside their school curriculum:

- **Elementary students (TK-5th grade):** Fun, interactive lessons emphasizing listening, correct pronunciation, essential vocabulary, songs, and games.
- **Middle and high school students:** Targeted grammar reinforcement, reading and writing development, homework help, and preparation for high school Spanish courses or AP Spanish exams.
- **Safety and background checks:** Instructors working with minors follow our [background check policy](/background-check-policy/).

### Charter school families {#charter}

Private Spanish lessons are an approved service for charter school families enrolled with [Pacific Coast Academy](https://pacificcoastacademy.org/) and [SoCal Scholars Academy](https://socalscholarsacademy.net/) across TK through 12th grade. Charter funds can cover lessons in person or online. [Contact us](/contact/) and name your charter school to get started.

## Private Lessons for Adults {#adults}

For adult learners, one-on-one sessions offer complete flexibility and practical, conversational focus:

- **Beginners:** Build essential grammar, everyday phrases, and conversational confidence from your very first class.
- **Intermediate & Advanced:** Fine-tune conversational fluency, expand your vocabulary, and explore cultural nuances and regional expressions.
- **Travel & Professional Spanish:** Practice real-world scenarios tailored to upcoming trips, healthcare, education, customer service, or business communication.
