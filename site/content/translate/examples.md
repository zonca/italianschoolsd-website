## Italian marriage certificate translation example {#marriage-certificate-example}

Want to see how a marriage certificate can look after professional translation into Italian? This **illustrative facsimile** demonstrates how numbered fields, names, dates, signatures, and registration sections can be organized in a clearly formatted document.

For a memorable example, we used **Giuseppe Garibaldi and Ana Maria “Anita” de Jesus Ribeiro da Silva**, who married in **Montevideo, Uruguay, on March 26, 1842**. The example includes their historical birth dates, but every administrative identifier, address, official designation, and fictional signature is made up.

**This is a teaching sample, not an authentic historical marriage certificate or a certified translation.** No real client information is included. The template is inspired by the layout of a US marriage certificate; Garibaldi's marriage did not take place under this US system.

For historical context, read [Giuseppe Garibaldi on Wikipedia (English)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Garibaldi) or the [Italian Presidency's historical archive on the couple's wedding](https://archivio.quirinale.it/aspr/gianni-bisiach/AV-002-000137/26-marzo-1842-matrimonio-giuseppe-e-anita-garibaldi).
