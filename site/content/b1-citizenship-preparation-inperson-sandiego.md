We offer specific preparation for the B1 citizenship test.

Before taking our B1 citizenship test preparation classes, we advise students to first take our regular in-person group classes: Beginner, Beginner-Intermediate, and Intermediate.

Students who are consistent and determined can progress one level per quarter (though normally it takes 1 quarter for Beginner, 2 quarters for Beginner-Intermediate, and 2 quarters for Intermediate).

Because the abilities of every student are different, we highly recommend taking private classes for the test preparation. We will build a customized plan with a specific number of lessons to ensure the student is ready for the test. These private classes are offered both in-person and online.

*Please note: We do not offer the B1 citizenship test itself at our location.*
