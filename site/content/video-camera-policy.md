Effective date: November 29, 2026

Italian school of San Diego LLC uses video cameras at its Kearny Mesa school to
protect the safety and security of students, families, teachers, staff and
property. This policy explains where the cameras are, what they record, who can
see the footage and how it is used. It applies to everyone who works, studies or
visits at the Kearny Mesa school.

## Where the cameras are

There are five indoor cameras, one in each of these rooms: Kitchen, Door
(entrance room), Studio, Main 1 and Play Room. Every camera is in plain view and
its status light stays on. There are no hidden cameras.

The video doorbell at the entrance door records only when someone rings the
bell.

No camera is placed in, or aimed at, a restroom, a place used for changing
clothes, or a space used for lactation. If a room with a camera is used for
lactation, the camera in that room is switched off for as long as the room is
used for that purpose. To arrange this, follow the Lactation Accommodation
Policy; the request can be made in any way that policy allows.

## What the cameras record

- **Video only.** The indoor cameras do not record sound, their microphones are
  switched off, and their speakers are switched off, so no one can listen to or
  talk into a room through them.
- **Motion-triggered clips.** The cameras record short clips when they detect a
  person, not continuous video.
- **No face recognition.** Face recognition and other identification features
  are switched off.
- **The doorbell** records video and sound only when someone rings the bell.

## How footage is used

Footage is used **only for safety and security**: for example, to understand an
accident or injury, a concern about a child's safety, a break-in, theft or
damage, or a dispute about something that happened at school.

Footage is **not** used to evaluate teaching, to monitor work performance or
attendance, or for routine supervision. It is reviewed only when there is a
specific safety or security reason. If footage reviewed for a safety or security
reason shows conduct that endangers a student or another person, the School may
use it to address that conduct and to make any report required by law.

The School does not use the cameras to watch or record employees discussing
wages, hours or other working conditions with each other.

## Who can see footage

Only Maura D'Andrea, Managing Member, and Andrea Zonca, Director of Operations,
can view live or recorded video. Teachers, students and families do not have
access to the cameras.

Footage is not posted, shared on social media, or given to anyone else, except:

- to law enforcement or a government agency when the law requires it or when
  needed to report a crime or a concern about a child's safety;
- to an insurer or attorney handling a claim about an incident; or
- as required by a court order or other legal process.

A family or staff member involved in an incident may ask to view the relevant
footage with the School. Copies are not given out when they show other
students.

## Storage and retention

Recordings are stored on a memory card inside each camera. They are not stored
in a cloud service, and app notifications are text only, without images.
Recordings are overwritten automatically as each card fills. When an incident
occurs, the relevant footage is saved separately and kept for as long as needed
to resolve it or as required by law.

## Questions and concerns

Anyone may ask questions about this policy or raise a concern by emailing
**andrea@italianschoolsd.com**. No one will be retaliated against for asking a
question, raising a concern, or objecting to this policy.
