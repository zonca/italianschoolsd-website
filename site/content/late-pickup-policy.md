Effective date: October 1, 2026

Parents or authorized caregivers are expected to pick up children promptly at the scheduled end of the program.

## Late pickup {#late-pickup}

A **10-minute grace period** is provided at no charge.

After the 10-minute grace period, a **late pickup fee of $20 for every additional 10 minutes, or any portion thereof** will apply.

Examples:

* Pickup within 10 minutes after dismissal: **No fee**
* 11-20 minutes late: **$20**
* 21-30 minutes late: **$40**
* 31-40 minutes late: **$60**

Calling, texting, or otherwise notifying the school on the same day that you will be late does not convert the late pickup into extended care.

## Pre-arranged extended care {#extended-care}

Families who know in advance that they need a later pickup time may request extended care.

Extended care:

* Must be requested **at least 24 hours in advance**
* Is subject to **staff availability and school approval**
* Is charged at **$25 per 30 minutes, or any portion thereof**
* Is not guaranteed until approved by the school

Examples:

* Up to 30 additional minutes: **$25**
* 31-60 additional minutes: **$50**
* 61-90 additional minutes: **$75**

Requests made with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to the standard late pickup policy rather than the extended-care rate.
