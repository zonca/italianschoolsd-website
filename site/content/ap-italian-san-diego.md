## AP Italian Overview {#overview}

> **AP Italian 2027 Preparation:** In-person exam preparation sessions meet in San Diego from January through May 2027 (30 hours across 9 Saturdays). The redesigned exam is on **Monday, May 3, 2027**. Read the full [AP Italian 2027 Exam Preparation details]({{< ref "news/ap-italian-2027-exam-preparation-in-person-sessions-in-san-diego.md" >}}).

Several High Schools in San Diego give the students the possibility of taking
AP exams, generally once a year in May.

One of the offerings of CollegeBoard is:

* [AP Italian Language and Culture](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-italian-language-and-culture/exam)

Students can ask their counselor to take the AP Italian test anytime between grade 9th and grade 12th, even
if the High School doesn't offer an AP Italian class.

Most Colleges give credits for AP tests, for example [University of California grants 8 quarters for AP Italian](https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/admission-requirements/ap-exam-credits/ap-credits/) passed with a score of 3 or higher.

Italian school of San Diego students which have reached year 4 of the [high school credit classes](/high-school-credit-classes) will have a wide knowledge of Italian that will allow them to take the exam with confidence.

It is important to stress that it is the cumulative instructions from the 4 levels
of Italian classes that prepare the students for the AP test, many topics useful
for the AP test are covered across levels 1-2, 3-4, and 5-6 (Italian as a New Language or Italian for Italian Speakers).

## In-Person Group Preparation (AP Italian 2027) {#group-prep}

We offer a dedicated **in-person AP Italian 2027 preparation program** (30 hours across 9 Saturdays, January to May 2027) at our Kearny Mesa location, specifically designed around the College Board's new redesigned digital exam format and Course Project requirements.

In 2026, **100% of our AP Italian students scored a 4 or 5** (two thirds earning the top score of 5).

* [Learn more about AP Italian 2027 In-Person Preparation]({{< ref "news/ap-italian-2027-exam-preparation-in-person-sessions-in-san-diego.md" >}})

## Private Tutoring and Dedicated Preparation {#private-prep}

For students who need a more personalized approach or want to focus specifically on the exam's technical expectations, we offer **private, one-on-one preparation sessions** with our School Director.

These sessions are available both online and in person and are specifically designed to address the challenges of the AP Italian exam, including the new digital format and Course Project requirements.
