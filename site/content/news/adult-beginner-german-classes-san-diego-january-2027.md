Our [World Languages program](/world-languages/) offers an in-person beginner German class for adults in Kearny Mesa starting on **Tuesday, January 19, 2027**.

The class is designed for adults with little or no prior knowledge of German. It is taught mostly in English, gradually adding more German as the course progresses. The curriculum is developed with the support of [German Pacific School San Diego](/german-classes-san-diego/#german-pacific-school).

It meets **Tuesdays from 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM**, January 19 to March 23, 2027. The course includes ten 90-minute classes. Tuition is $380 in full or three monthly payments of $139.40.

Enroll by January 14, 2027.

## Beginner German for adults {#german-beg}

Build a practical foundation in German through conversation, listening, vocabulary, reading, writing, and grammar.

- **Schedule:** Tuesdays, 6:00 PM-7:30 PM
- **Dates:** January 19 to March 23, 2027 (10 classes)
- **Location:** [4550 Kearny Villa Rd, Suite 202, San Diego](/location/) (free on-site parking, easy access from I-805, SR-163, and SR-52)
- **Tuition:** $380 in full or three monthly payments of $139.40
- **Enroll by:** January 14, 2027
- **Textbook:** *Schritte International Neu 1 (A1.1)*, Hueber Verlag (ISBN 978-3-19-361082-9)

### Policies and discounts {#policies}

- **Family discount:** For full-payment enrollment in the same class, the first student pays full tuition and each additional student from the same family receives 10% off automatically.
- **Monthly plan:** The three-payment plan costs $418.20 total (three payments of $139.40), including a 10% administrative fee. It covers one student per checkout and requires all three payments.
- **Student cancellations:** Full refund if canceled at least 15 days before the first class; 50% refund after that deadline but before the second class; no refunds after the second class begins.
