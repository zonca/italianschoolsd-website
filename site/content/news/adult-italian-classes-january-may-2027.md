Continue your Italian with weekly, 90-minute group classes in **Kearny Mesa**, **Encinitas**, or **online via Zoom**. The session begins the week of **January 11, 2027** and finishes the week of **May 17**, alongside our regular children's programs.

Returning groups continue with the same teacher and progress to the next level. New students with prior Italian experience are welcome: [tell us about your background]({{< relref "contact.md" >}}) so we can help you find a suitable group. **Advanced conversation** is the proposed continuation of Ilana's conversation group.

## Tuition and payment options {#tuition}

{{< adult-january-may-2027-rates >}} Enrollment covers the full session; the total depends on the number of lessons in your section, listed below. All classes last 90 minutes.

You can pay in full or choose **five monthly payments**, including the existing **10% administrative fee**. The monthly plan is a commitment to the entire session. Textbooks are separate; your teacher will confirm the materials for your level.

In-person classes meet at our [Kearny Mesa location]({{< relref "location.md" >}}) or at [170 Calle Magdalena in Encinitas]({{< relref "italian-classes-encinitas.md" >}}#location). All times below are **Pacific Time**. The two Friday Encinitas sections have separate teachers and enrollment options.

## Holidays and spring break {#holidays}

Monday classes skip January 18, February 15, and March 8. Friday classes skip February 12. All sections take spring break during the week of **March 29-April 4**, including the Saturday class on April 3.

The last lessons are May 17 for Monday groups, May 18 for Tuesday, May 19 for Wednesday, May 20 for Thursday, May 21 for Friday, and May 22 for Saturday. Each section's complete date list appears above.

## Enrollment policies {#policies}

For pay-in-full enrollment in the same class, the first student pays full tuition and additional students from the same family receive 10% off automatically. Monthly checkout is for one student at a time.

See our [adult-class policies, refunds, and discounts]({{< relref "adults.md" >}}#policies) before enrolling.
