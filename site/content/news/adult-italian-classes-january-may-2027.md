Continue your Italian with weekly, 90-minute group classes in **Kearny Mesa**, **Encinitas**, or **online via Zoom**. The session begins the week of **January 11, 2027** and finishes the week of **May 17**, alongside our regular children's programs.

Returning groups generally continue with the same teacher and progress to the next level. Encinitas offers one **beginner** section with Daniela and one **beginner-intermediate** section with Laura. New **beginner sections meet Wednesdays in Kearny Mesa and Thursdays online**. Students with prior Italian experience are welcome: [tell us about your background]({{< relref "contact.md" >}}) so we can help you find a suitable group. Ilana's group remains **Conversation**.

## Tuition and payment options {#tuition}

{{< adult-january-may-2027-rates >}} Enrollment covers the full session; the total depends on the number of lessons in your section, listed below. All classes last 90 minutes.

You can pay in full or choose **five monthly payments**, including the existing **10% administrative fee**. The monthly plan is a commitment to the entire session.

**Payment starts January 2, 2027 (Pacific Time).** If you enroll earlier, Stripe securely saves your card and schedules either your full tuition or the first of five monthly payments for January 2. Monthly payments then follow on February 2, March 2, April 2, and May 2. If you enroll on or after January 2, your full tuition or first monthly payment is charged immediately; monthly payments then follow your enrollment date. Textbooks are separate; your teacher will confirm the materials for your level.

In-person classes meet at our [Kearny Mesa location]({{< relref "location.md" >}}) or at [170 Calle Magdalena in Encinitas]({{< relref "italian-classes-encinitas.md" >}}#location). All times below are **Pacific Time**. The two Friday Encinitas sections have separate teachers and enrollment options.

For pay-in-full enrollment in the same class, the first student pays full tuition and additional students from the same family receive 10% off automatically. Monthly checkout is for one student at a time.
