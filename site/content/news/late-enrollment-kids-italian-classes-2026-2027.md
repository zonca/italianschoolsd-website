## Programs open for 2026-2027 {#classes}

* **Italian as a New Language (new learners), TK-6th grade**
  Wednesdays, **4:00-6:00pm**, August 26, 2026-May 19, 2027
* **Italian for Italian Speakers (advanced or fluent children), TK-6th grade**
  Thursdays, **4:15-6:15pm**, August 27, 2026-May 20, 2027
* **Morning program for homeschoolers**, ages 4 and older
  Wednesdays, **10am-12pm**, September 2, 2026-May 19, 2027

Classes have **5-10 students** and are grouped by age and level, so your child will be placed with peers at the same stage. All our teachers are native Italian speakers.

For students in 7th grade and above, see our [high school credit classes for teens](/high-school-credit-classes).

## Where {#location}

## Late enrollment tuition {#tuition}

Tuition is prorated for the remaining classes. See the [current late-enrollment rates and applicable fees]({{< relref "enroll.md#tuition" >}}) and [proration calculation]({{< relref "tuition-payment.md#late-enrollments" >}}).

We are an approved vendor for [Pacific Coast Academy](https://pacificcoastacademy.org/) and [SoCal Scholars Academy](https://socalscholarsacademy.net/), so charter funds can be used for our classes. If your child attends another charter, [contact us](/contact) and we can apply to become vendors.

## How to enroll {#enroll}

1. **Fill out the pre-enrollment form.** It takes two minutes and requires no payment.
2. **We confirm the class.** We will email you the proposed class, the teacher, and the tuition details.
3. **Pay to finalize.** Payment is due within one week of receiving the confirmation email.

Pre-enrollment is not binding, and we will help you choose the right level before you commit.

Afternoon classes began the week of **August 26, 2026**, and the morning program began **September 2, 2026**. Students may still join a suitable class during the academic year, pending availability. [Contact us](/contact) to check placement and prorated tuition.
