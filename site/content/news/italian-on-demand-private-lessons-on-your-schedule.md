A weekly class is the best way to make steady progress in Italian, but not every week looks the same. Work trips, family schedules and the holidays get in the way. **Italian On Demand** is our answer: private Italian lessons online via Zoom that you book only when you want one.

## Book when it suits you {#flexible}

- **Pick any open slot** on the booking calendar, as little as **12 hours in advance**.
- **One lesson at a time:** no semester, no recurring schedule and no long-term commitment. Book one lesson this week and the next one whenever you are ready.
- **Learn from anywhere:** every lesson is online via Zoom, so you can join from home, from the office or while traveling.
- **Alone or with a partner:** book a lesson for one person, or for two people learning together.
- **Instant confirmation:** after payment you automatically receive a calendar invite with the Zoom link.

## A lesson built around you {#tailored}

Each lesson lasts one hour and is taught by a native Italian teacher. After you book, you tell us about your Italian so far and what you want to work on, and your teacher prepares the lesson around it. Students use Italian On Demand to:

- start Italian from scratch with a teacher one-on-one
- get extra speaking practice alongside one of our weekly classes
- prepare for a trip to Italy
- review grammar or polish pronunciation
- talk with family in Italian, or prepare for citizenship, school or work

We teach every level, from complete beginner to advanced.

## Five-class packages, for you or as a gift {#packages}

If you already know you want several lessons, a **five-class package** gives you five credits to use one at a time, whenever an open slot works for you. **Credits do not expire**, so there is no pressure to fit them into a set number of weeks.

Packages are also a thoughtful holiday gift for anyone who loves Italy: the buyer receives a package code by email, and the recipient books each lesson on their own schedule.
