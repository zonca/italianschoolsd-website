The Italian School of San Diego is pleased to confirm the continuation of our children's Spanish language program for the 2026-2027 academic year!

This in-person course is designed specifically for **3rd year students** (elementary school students who have completed approximately two years of beginner Spanish instruction, or continuing learners with comparable foundational skills). Meeting in a focused, small-group setting, children will continue advancing their speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills through structured lessons, creative activities, cultural stories, and games.

## Schedule {#schedule}

* **Day and time:** Wednesdays, 2:30 PM - 4:00 PM (1.5 hours per class)
* **First lesson:** Wednesday, September 23, 2026
* **Last lesson:** Wednesday, May 19, 2027
* **Total instruction:** 29 weeks (43.5 total classroom hours)

## Instructor & Curriculum {#instructor}

Instruction is led by our new Spanish instructor, **Edith**. Edith is a native Spanish speaker holding a Bachelor's Degree in Language Teaching, a Master's Degree in Modern Languages, and Cambridge English TKT certification. She brings extensive experience teaching children in elementary and middle school settings as well as adult learners.

### Textbook

The class will use the **Lola y Leo 3** series (Libro del alumno and Cuaderno de ejercicios, published by Difusión). Books will be available for pickup directly at our school or can be purchased online.
