You do not need to wait for the start of a new academic year or session to ask about joining an Italian class.

> **We accept enrollment requests throughout the year for all our kids classes, pending availability, and for all our adult Italian classes, pending a placement assessment.**

## Kids and teen classes: pending availability {#kids-classes}

Enrollment requests remain open throughout the year for all our classes for children and teens, from transitional kindergarten through high school. This includes Italian as a New Language, Italian for Italian Speakers, homeschool morning classes, and high school credit classes.

Placement depends on space in a class that fits the student's age and Italian level. If a suitable class has space, we provide the class details and prorated late-enrollment tuition before the family commits.

## Adult classes: pending assessment {#adult-classes}

We also accept enrollment requests throughout the year for all our adult Italian classes. Before recommending a class, we assess the student's current Italian level, prior experience, goals, and the progress of the available sections.

The assessment helps us determine whether a student can join a current class successfully or whether a different section or upcoming session would be a better fit.
